結合生成式人工智慧與地理資訊之
智慧旅遊行程規劃系統

大學專題研究報告

中文摘要

隨著生成式人工智慧（Generative Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI） 與地理資訊技術（Geographic Information System, GIS）的快速發展， 人工智慧逐漸應用於旅遊規劃、資訊搜尋與個人化推薦等領域。 傳統旅遊行程規劃通常需要使用者自行搜尋景點、查詢交通資訊並安排景點順序， 不僅耗費時間，也可能因景點距離、營業時間及行程時間等因素造成規劃上的困難。

因此，本研究旨在開發一套結合生成式人工智慧與地理資訊之智慧旅遊行程規劃系統， 以協助使用者快速產生符合個人需求的旅遊行程。 本系統透過使用者輸入旅遊地點、旅遊日期、旅遊天數、預算、交通方式及興趣偏好等條件， 結合生成式人工智慧進行自然語言理解與行程內容生成， 再搭配地理資訊技術分析景點位置、距離及行程順序， 產生具備時間安排與地理位置資訊的旅遊規劃結果。

系統亦可將規劃完成的景點與行程以地圖方式呈現， 使使用者能夠更直觀地了解旅遊路線與景點分布。 本研究期望透過生成式人工智慧與地理資訊技術的整合， 降低使用者規劃旅遊行程所需的時間與操作成本， 並提升行程規劃的便利性與個人化程度。

關鍵詞： 生成式人工智慧、地理資訊系統、智慧旅遊、行程規劃、個人化推薦

Abstract

With the rapid development of Generative Artificial Intelligence (Generative AI) and Geographic Information System (GIS) technologies, artificial intelligence has been increasingly applied to travel planning, information retrieval, and personalized recommendation services.

Traditional travel planning usually requires users to search for attractions, transportation information, opening hours, and suitable routes by themselves. This process can be time-consuming and may result in inefficient itineraries due to factors such as geographical distance, travel time, and attraction schedules.

Therefore, this study aims to develop an intelligent travel itinerary planning system that integrates Generative AI with geographic information technology to assist users in efficiently creating personalized travel itineraries.

The proposed system allows users to provide travel-related requirements, including destination, travel dates, duration, budget, transportation methods, and personal interests. Generative AI is utilized to understand the user's requirements and generate appropriate itinerary content. Geographic information technology is integrated to analyze the locations and distances between attractions and to arrange an appropriate visiting sequence.

The system also presents the planned attractions and routes on a digital map, allowing users to clearly understand the geographical distribution of attractions and the overall travel route.

Keywords: Generative Artificial Intelligence, Geographic Information System, Smart Tourism, Travel Itinerary Planning, Personalized Recommendation
